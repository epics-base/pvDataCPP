Detailed Description

A semi-hack to help with migration from std::auto_ptr to std::unique_ptr, and avoid copious deprecation warning spam which may be hiding legitimate issues.

Provides epics::auto_ptr<T> and epics::swap()

epics::auto_ptr<T> is std::auto_ptr<T> for c++98 and std::unique_ptr<T> for >= c++11.

epics::swap() is the only supported operation. copy/assignment/return are not supported (use auto_ptr or unique_ptr explicitly).
